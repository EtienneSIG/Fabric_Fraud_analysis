# KYC Refresh

**Domain:** KYC · **Typology:** kyc-refresh · **Role:** distractor (synthetic)

## Definition

Know-Your-Customer (KYC) refresh is the periodic re-verification of customer identity,
beneficial ownership and risk profile after onboarding. It keeps the customer record
current so monitoring baselines stay meaningful. It is an onboarding/lifecycle control, not
a money-laundering typology — present here as an AML distractor with a genuine adjacency.

## Refresh triggers

- Time-based cycle by risk band (High annually, Medium every 2–3 years, Low longer).
- Event-based: change of address, ownership, activity pattern or adverse media hit.
- Regulatory or sanctions-list changes affecting the customer.

## Adjacency to AML

KYC refresh *feeds* AML: a stale profile weakens the "inconsistent with expected activity"
assessment in a SAR narrative (see `../aml/customer-risk-rating.md`). But refreshing KYC is
not itself a laundering typology. Keep the distinction in any answer.
