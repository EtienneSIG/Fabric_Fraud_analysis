# Enhanced Due Diligence Triggers

**Domain:** KYC · **Typology:** edd-triggers · **Role:** distractor (synthetic)

## Definition

Enhanced due diligence (EDD) is the deeper investigation applied to higher-risk customers
and relationships. It goes beyond standard due diligence with source-of-wealth
verification, senior sign-off and closer ongoing monitoring.

## Triggers

- High customer risk rating (see `../aml/customer-risk-rating.md`).
- PEP status or adverse media (see `../aml/pep-screening.md`).
- Higher-risk jurisdictions, products or correspondent relationships.
- Unusual ownership structures or opaque beneficial ownership.

## Adjacency to AML

EDD is a **control intensity**, not a typology. It changes how closely you watch, not what
you are watching for. In a SAR narrative, EDD status explains why scrutiny was heightened;
it does not replace naming the laundering typology. Distractor for AML retrieval.
