# Synthetic Identity Fraud

**Domain:** Identity · **Typology:** synthetic-identity · **Role:** distractor (synthetic)

## Definition

Synthetic identity fraud fabricates a new identity by combining real and fictitious
information (for example a real national ID number with an invented name and date of birth)
to open accounts and build credit before defaulting. The identity does not correspond to a
single real person.

## Signals

- Thin or inconsistent credit history that grows unusually fast.
- Shared attributes (address, phone) across many "distinct" identities.
- Identity elements that fail cross-source consistency checks.

## Adjacency to AML

Synthetic identities can be the account-holders used in **smurfing** networks
(`../aml/smurfing.md`), but synthetic-identity fraud is an identity typology, not a
laundering typology. Present as an AML distractor; do not conflate the two in a narrative.
