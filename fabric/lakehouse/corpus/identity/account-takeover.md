# Account Takeover

**Domain:** Identity · **Typology:** account-takeover · **Role:** distractor (synthetic)

## Definition

Account takeover (ATO) is the unauthorised capture of a legitimate customer's account
through stolen credentials, SIM-swap, phishing or malware. The attacker then transacts as
the genuine customer. The distinguishing feature is that a **real account is hijacked**,
not fabricated.

## Signals

- Credential change (password, phone, email) immediately followed by high-value activity.
- Login from a new device or improbable geography.
- Payee added then large transfer within a short window.

## Adjacency to AML

Funds extracted via ATO may subsequently be **layered**, but ATO itself is an identity /
access typology, not a laundering technique. Included as an AML distractor. Name the
laundering typology separately if downstream movement is observed.
