# Provider Collusion

**Domain:** Claims · **Typology:** provider-collusion · **Role:** distractor (synthetic)

## Definition

Provider collusion is organised insurance fraud in which repair shops, clinics or other
service providers conspire with claimants (or operate rings) to inflate or fabricate
claims. Detection centres on provider concentration and network analysis.

## Signals

- A small set of providers appearing across a disproportionate share of claims.
- Repeated claimant-provider pairings beyond chance.
- Billing patterns clustered around policy limits.

## Why this is only a distractor

Provider collusion is a **graph-centrality / concentration problem**, closer to the entity-
graph screen than to document retrieval, and it is insurance, not AML. It is present as a
distractor only; the collusion-network domain is explicitly out of RAFT scope.
