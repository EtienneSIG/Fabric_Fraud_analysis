# SAR Filing Procedure

**Domain:** Procedures · **Typology:** sar-filing · **Role:** procedure (synthetic)

## Purpose

The operational steps to file a suspicious activity report once a case is assessed as
SAR-ready. This is the *procedure*; the *readiness assessment* lives in
`../aml/sar-filing-guidance.md`. The system never files automatically — a human authoriser
must approve.

## Steps

1. Confirm the case is SAR-ready: typology named, rule cited, pattern grounded, assessment
   complete (see the five-part narrative).
2. Nominated officer reviews the narrative and the exculpatory analysis.
3. Authoriser approves or returns for more evidence.
4. On approval, file with the relevant financial intelligence unit within the mandated
   window, in the required format.
5. Record the filing reference, decision and rationale in the audit trail; avoid tipping
   off the subject.

## Regulatory framing (original summary)

Reporting obligations require timely filing on reasonable suspicion and retention of the
supporting rationale. Summarise obligations in your own words; never reproduce published
regulatory text verbatim.
