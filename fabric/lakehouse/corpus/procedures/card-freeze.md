# Card Freeze Procedure

**Domain:** Procedures · **Typology:** card-freeze · **Role:** procedure (synthetic)

## Purpose

Operational steps to freeze a card or account when fraud is suspected. Cross-domain and
reversible; a freeze is a containment action, not a final decision.

## Steps

1. Confirm the suspicion basis and record it.
2. Apply a temporary block through the card/account management system.
3. Notify the customer through a verified channel; never through the channel under attack.
4. Open or link a case for investigation.
5. Review within the defined window; lift or extend the block with authoriser sign-off.

## Note

A freeze buys investigation time; it does not conclude the case. For AML matters, avoid
tipping off the subject — coordinate with the nominated officer before any customer
contact. See `case-escalation.md` and `../aml/sar-filing-guidance.md`.
